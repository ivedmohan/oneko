# Asset credits

- `neko.png`, `tora.png`, `dog.png`, `sakura.png`, `tomoyo.png` are generated by `scripts/xbm2sheet.py` from the
  XBM bitmaps in the original `oneko-1.2.sakura.6` source (Debian's `oneko` orig tarball). Those bitmaps are
  public domain: neko/tora/dog copyright 1990 Tatsuya Kato, sakura/tomoyo copyright 1990 Kiichiroh Mukose.
- `oneko.gif` is the sprite sheet from [adryd325/oneko.js](https://github.com/adryd325/oneko.js), MIT licensed, copyright 2022 adryd. It is only used by a unit test now; the app ships the generated sheets.
- The cat itself is from the original X11 `oneko` (1990, Tatsuya Kato, based on Masayuki Koba's `xneko`), which is public domain.
- The movement and idle behaviour in `src/cat.rs` is a port of the state machine in `oneko.js`.
